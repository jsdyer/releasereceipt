      What counts as a revision?

      Each bulletin gives six consecutive months of seasonally adjusted real GDP growth relative to the preceding month, rounded to one decimal place. The newest month is a preliminary estimate. Later tables can reflect new survey information and seasonal adjustments. A move from +0.3% to +0.4% is a revision of +0.1 percentage points.

      The archive follows the regular monthly tables. Advance headline estimates and later benchmark revisions outside the six-column window are separate. Zero includes published values rounded to positive or negative zero. A missing first table means the archive starts after that month’s original release.
